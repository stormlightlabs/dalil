Week of 2026-11-02 to 2026-11-08, re-planned on 2026-11-04 around primitives that serve people and agents.

The replay harness (#9) and an agent A/B showed context and search losing to a keyword baseline and to grep. This sprint builds the primitives first: ranked multi-word search with lines and snippets, a usages command, past-commit and co-change history, then context rebuilt from them and scored by the harness. Interning, the postcard store, the commit table, the MCP naming decision and the release flow move to the next sprint.
